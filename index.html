How it works

1

Read the load

A smart plug or meter reader sends each appliance’s wattage. In this demo that feed is simulated.

2

Price every habit

Watts × hours × your tariff slab gives the rupee cost of each appliance, and shows when a slab jump is coming.

3

Apply the plan

Rules suggest timers, setpoints and off-peak shifts. Switch the plan on to see the new bill and the lower evening peak.
